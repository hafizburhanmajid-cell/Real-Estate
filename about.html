Our story

Started in one office above a bakery.
Still answering our own phones.

LUXE REALTY opened in 2009 with two advisors and a conviction that property advice should sound like a conversation, not a pitch. Seventeen years later we work across four cities and still cap each advisor's listings so nobody becomes a number on a spreadsheet.

We photograph every home ourselves, walk every neighbourhood before we describe it, and tell clients when a property is wrong for them — which is, more often than not, why they come back.
